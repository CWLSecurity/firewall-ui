Large Transfer Delay (Safe)

Vault / Base Pack 0

Policy address: 0x3217E8641b6d5FA4031ee5AF15FB7716CFD16adf

What This Means For Users

  • In short: large transfers do not execute instantly.
  • When it triggers: when transfer amount is at or above threshold.
  • What you see in UI: transfer goes to queue with unlock time.
  • Why it matters: gives time to stop a mistaken or malicious outflow.

Simple Mental Model

Think of it as a safety pause before moving meaningful funds.

Technical details
  • policyName: LargeTransferDelayPolicy
  • policyKey: 0xbef8b32671d12321edc6cf9fdd5ca723cb22142120e78b804bd3a169db17e54b
  • policyConfigVersion: 1
  • eth_threshold_wei: 10 ETH
  • erc20_threshold_units: 10 ETH-equivalent raw units
  • delay_seconds: 3600 (1 hour)
  • comparator_mode: gte
  • selector_scope: eth+erc20xfers

New Spender Approval Delay

DeFi Trader / Base Pack 1

Policy address: 0xAb25377bdd4147F309E28f9dcE8cCEe8D37932e1

What This Means For Users

  • In short: first approval to a new spender is delayed.
  • When it triggers: first non-zero approve to a new contract spender.
  • What you see in UI: queued approval; approvals to EOAs may be rejected.
  • Why it matters: reduces fast-drain risk through new spender contracts.

Simple Mental Model

New spenders start with friction until they become known.

  • policyConfigVersion: 1
  • delay_seconds: 1800 (30 minutes)
  • known_scope: vault_token_spender
  • eoa_nonzero_action: revert
  • new_contract_action: delay
