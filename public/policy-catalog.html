Large Transfer Delay (Vault)

Vault / Base Pack 0

Policy address: 0xA70C50724Ed2b6b8c2234BE9b56D5987A519ED75

What This Means For Users

  • In short: larger transfers are paused before they can go through.
  • When it triggers: when native ETH or ERC-20 transfer amount is at or above the configured threshold.
  • What you see in UI: transfer goes to queue with unlock time.
  • Why it matters: gives time to stop a mistaken or malicious outflow.

Simple Mental Model

Think of it as a review window before moving a meaningful amount out of the Vault.

Technical details
  • policyName: LargeTransferDelayPolicy
  • policyKey: 0xbef8b32671d12321edc6cf9fdd5ca723cb22142120e78b804bd3a169db17e54b
  • policyConfigVersion: 2
  • eth_threshold_wei: 10 ETH
  • erc20_threshold_units: 10 ETH-equivalent raw units
  • delay_seconds: 3600 (1 hour)
  • comparator_mode: gte
  • selector_scope: eth+erc20xfers

New Receiver Delay (Vault)

Vault / Base Pack 0

Policy address: 0xdbb505D9487c699e3Ba75651e8D80350B8B606B6

What This Means For Users

  • In short: the first transfer to a new address is delayed.
  • When it triggers: when the Vault has not sent to that address before, whether the destination is a personal wallet or a contract.
  • What you see in UI: queued transfer with a timer.
  • Why it matters: helps catch address mistakes before funds leave.

Simple Mental Model

The first payment to a new destination gets a built-in pause for review.

Technical details
  • policyName: NewReceiverDelayPolicy
  • policyKey: 0x1bd0c2dce1f6acb89d6f11a0e04e91154710d9d43725e31a799e90dd7d6e7981
  • policyConfigVersion: 1
  • delay_seconds: 3600 (1 hour)
  • eoa_only: false
  • receiver_scope: all_receivers

DeFi Approval Mode

DeFi Trader / Base Pack 1

Policy address: 0xC30aA816F27D19F57Fb25CbA48B93944BcC4b23c

What This Means For Users

  • In short: most common DeFi approvals stay usable, but one broad approval pattern is blocked.
  • When it triggers: on approve/permit/setApprovalForAll calls.
  • What you see in UI: common DeFi approvals usually proceed, but broad operator approvals are blocked.
  • Why it matters: balances usability and safety for active DeFi usage.

Simple Mental Model

This mode is meant to feel workable for active DeFi use without removing approval safety entirely.

Technical details
  • policyName: DeFiApprovalPolicy
  • policyKey: 0x87b150af2a05b56005ea04e94fb5db71f5e80f9642927af6deef08e9acd06635
  • policyConfigVersion: 1
  • allow_max_approval: true
  • allow_permit: true
  • block_setapproval_true: true

New Spender Approval Delay

DeFi Trader / Base Pack 1

Policy address: 0x490306D6f6230CD0AD65B9dAC7d1fad391107694

What This Means For Users

  • In short: the first meaningful approval to a new spender is slowed down.
  • When it triggers: first non-zero approval to a new contract spender.
  • What you see in UI: a queued approval for a new contract spender; non-zero approvals to personal wallet addresses are blocked.
  • Why it matters: reduces fast-drain risk through new spender contracts.

Simple Mental Model

A new spender does not get trusted immediately.

Technical details
  • policyName: ApprovalToNewSpenderDelayPolicy
  • policyKey: 0x6414c51801a5243dd8022e758af090f7847ab2e18a308a4bdca30ca64965f4e3
  • policyConfigVersion: 2
  • delay_seconds: 1800 (30 minutes)
  • known_scope: vault_token_spender
  • eoa_nonzero_action: revert
  • new_contract_action: delay

New EOA Receiver Delay

DeFi Trader / Base Pack 1

Policy address: 0xec5ef74f08D758DcEd8BF7afA033C692602DbCb0

What This Means For Users

  • In short: new personal wallet addresses and some unfamiliar contract actions get a review delay.
  • When it triggers: the first transfer to a new personal wallet address, and the first unfamiliar contract interaction pattern.
  • What you see in UI: a queued delay for a new personal wallet, or for a first unfamiliar contract interaction.
  • Why it matters: adds friction for risky personal-address sends and first-time contract interactions.

Simple Mental Model

New personal wallets are treated cautiously, and unfamiliar contract actions also get a first-pass review window.

24-Hour New Receiver Delay

Optional Add-on / Pack 2

Policy address: 0xCFe2d479612752fF7e885010b0E0887FF5860980

What This Means For Users

  • In short: the first transfer to a new address is delayed for 24 hours.
  • When it triggers: a recipient the Vault has never sent to before, including contracts.
  • Compared to base Vault: extends delay from 1 hour to 24 hours.
  • What you see in UI: long queue timer (24h).
  • Why it matters: maximum reaction window against address replacement attacks.

Simple Mental Model

This is the highest-friction option for brand-new destinations.

Technical details
  • policyName: NewReceiverDelayPolicy
  • policyKey: 0x1bd0c2dce1f6acb89d6f11a0e04e91154710d9d43725e31a799e90dd7d6e7981
  • policyConfigVersion: 1
  • delay_seconds: 86400 (24 hours)
  • eoa_only: false
  • receiver_scope: all_receivers
