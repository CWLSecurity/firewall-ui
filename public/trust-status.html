What Firewall Vault is designed to help with

  • Delaying first transfers to new destinations.
  • Delaying larger outflows before they can execute.
  • Blocking or delaying selected risky approval patterns.
  • Giving users a queue where delayed actions can be reviewed, executed, or canceled.

What it does not promise

  • It is not insurance and cannot recover funds after they leave.
  • It does not protect a leaked seed phrase or compromised device.
  • It is not universal dApp compatibility yet.
  • It does not remove the need to verify the site, contracts, and transaction details.

Security reporting

If you believe you found a vulnerability, report it privately before public disclosure. Do not send seed phrases, private keys, or funds.
